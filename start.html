📋 Hardware Checklist

You will need:

Minimum Requirements:

  • Computer: Any x86-64 PC, laptop, or mini-PC with 8GB+ RAM
  • Operating System: Windows 10/11 with WSL2, OR Ubuntu/Debian Linux
  • Storage: 100GB+ free disk space (500GB+ recommended for media)
  • Network: Wired ethernet connection recommended

Optional (for later units):

  • NAS: Synology, QNAP, or TrueNAS for centralized storage
  • Proxmox server: Dedicated hardware for VM/container management
  • Smart devices: Zigbee/Z-Wave hubs, smart lights, sensors for HA
  • Microphone/Speaker: USB mic or Raspberry Pi for voice assistant
💡 Starting simple? You can complete Units 1–2 (Docker + ARR) on a single Windows laptop with WSL2. Home Assistant and voice come later.

🧪 Course Philosophy

TRaSH First, Random YouTube Later

This course follows TRaSH Guides as the authoritative source for ARR media automation. TRaSH Guides are maintained alongside Sonarr/Radarr and stay current. YouTube videos are useful for seeing concepts, but the guides are your instruction manual.

Docker Compose, Not Click-Ops

You will learn to define your stack in docker-compose.yml files so you can rebuild your entire homelab from code. No mystery containers created by clicking through Portainer.

Understand → Build → Break → Fix → Verify

Every lesson includes:

  • Concept: What you're learning and WHY it matters
  • Lab: Hands-on steps to build it
  • Checkpoints: Verify it works before continuing
  • Troubleshooting: Decision trees for when things break
⚠️ No Shortcuts: If you skip the Docker networking lesson and jump straight to Gluetun, you WILL get stuck. Follow the order.

🎥 About Lecture Videos

Throughout this course, you'll see references to YouTube videos from:

  • NetworkChuck — Docker, networking, VMs, home automation
  • IBRACORP — Prowlarr, TRaSH Guides + Notifiarr
  • Smart Home Junkie — Home Assistant beginner guides
  • Everything Smart Home — Local voice control

Important notes on videos:

  • Videos are lecture supplements, not the product. The lessons in this classroom are the lab.
  • Some videos were made in 2022–2024. UIs change. Always check official documentation for current steps.
  • Do NOT follow every video step-by-step if it conflicts with TRaSH or this course. Use videos to understand concepts, then follow the classroom labs.
